Foot massage & reflexology in Da Nang in Da Nang

The cheapest way to undo a day of walking a beach city.

What a foot massage usually includes

A warm soak, then work on the feet and calves in a reclining chair. Longer sessions often add the shoulders or a short head massage: check what the menu lists for the minutes.

Reflexology

Reflexology is pressure on points of the sole. It can be firm; say so if it hurts.

What it costs

Prices vary from house to house: ask for the menu, with the minutes stated, before you sit down.

Frequently asked

Is a foot massage done in a private room?

Often in a shared room of reclining chairs; ask the venue if you prefer a private room.
