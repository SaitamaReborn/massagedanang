Best massage in Da Nang: 10 venues scored out of 100 (November 2026)

Short answer

The first massage venue in Da Nang on our score out of 100 is Panda Spa Body & Foot Massage Danang (89.4/100). Every venue is scored on the same nine criteria a visitor can check before booking: the Google rating weighted by its number of reviews, a price list and durations on its own website, WhatsApp or online booking, English stated and confirmed, hygiene described, the range of treatments (body, foot, head spa, hot stone, Thai or shiatsu), opening hours, distance to My Khe beach and a website of its own. The weights are in the matrix and on the methodology page.

The top five: 1. Panda Spa Body & Foot Massage Danang (89.4), 2. B Spa Bamboo Viet (89.4), 3. Panda Relax Spa (89.2), 4. Reborn Nails & Retreat (86.2), 5. Nón Spa - Massage, Nail, Hair Washing, Facial and Skin care (82.3).

Our own count of 27 November 2026: of 238 massage venues, 77 publish a price list on a website of their own, 7 state there that English is spoken and 114 are within 1 km of My Khe beach; median published price of a 60-minute body massage: 420K VND (11 venues).

The first three are level. Panda Spa Body & Foot Massage and B Spa Bamboo Viet share the top score, and Panda Relax Spa is 0.2 behind: all three publish every treatment on the list with prices and minutes, take WhatsApp or online bookings, have English stated or confirmed, describe a hygiene practice and sit within 1 km of the beach. Panda Spa comes first on the tie-break, its larger number of reviews (3,183).

The first five, one by one

1. Panda Spa Body & Foot Massage

Hours, map and reviews on this guide · Google listing

229 Nguyễn Văn Thoại, An Hải, near the beach. Daily 9:00 to 23:45. Google: 4.9 from 3,183. Its menu lists foot massage, shiatsu, full-body massage with essential oils, Thai massage and hot stones; a 60-minute relaxing massage is 550,000 VND, tip included. A reviewer: “everyone speaks English very well”. Same company as Panda Relax Spa, on the same street.

2. B Spa Bamboo Viet

Hours, map and reviews on this guide · Google listing

1 Lê Quang Đạo, Ngũ Hành Sơn. Daily 8:00 to 1:00. Google: 4.9 from 1,547. Its site: “Our team and menus speak English, Vietnamese, Chinese and Korean”; a 60-minute traditional Vietnamese body massage with oil and acupressure is 420,000 VND; bamboo and hot stone massage on the same menu.

3. Panda Relax Spa

Hours, map and reviews on this guide · Google listing

225c Nguyễn Văn Thoại, An Hải. Daily 9:00 to 23:45. Google: 4.9 from 369. Same menu and prices as Panda Spa; its site states that “all tools, towels, and robes are thoroughly cleaned and sterilized after each use”. A reviewer: “Good English at reception”.

4. Reborn Nails & Retreat

Hours, map and reviews on this guide · Google listing

56 Châu Thị Vĩnh Tế, My An, 880 m from the beach. Daily 9:00 to 20:00. Google: 4.9 from 386. A nail salon and head spa whose menu prices a 60-minute full body massage (340,000 VND), foot and calf massage (15 or 30 minutes), herbal head spa rituals of 25 to 105 minutes and hot stones, with their minutes. Points lost: no Thai or shiatsu on its menu (3 points). On the four rankings by treatment (foot massage, head spa, hot stones, near the beach) it comes third.

5. Nón Spa

Hours, map and reviews on this guide · Google listing

43 An Thượng 3, An Thượng. Google: 4.9 from 704. Its menu lists Vietnamese, aroma, herbal, hot stone, four-hands and Thai massage; its FAQ: “English of our receptionists are quite good”. Points lost: no hygiene practice described on its site, and Google lists its hours for fewer than seven days.
