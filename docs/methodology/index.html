How this guide scores massage venues

Who is in it

Every massage venue in Da Nang with a public Google rating and at least twenty reviews, from the Google Places API (snapshot of 27 November 2026, 238 venues): venues whose Google category is a massage or spa category, and venues of another care category whose own website lists a body or foot massage. Hotel listings are left out because their Google rating is for the hotel, not for its spa; a hotel spa with its own listing is in.

The score out of 100

Every venue is scored on the same nine criteria, chosen for a visitor who speaks English, stays near the beach and wants a treatment without a surprise at the counter. The weights were set by the editors on 27 November 2026, before any venue was scored, and are the same for everyone. A criterion with nothing readable scores 0. Facebook and Instagram pages cannot be read without an account, so a venue whose only page is there scores 0 on the criteria that need a website.

Google figures are the snapshot of 27 November 2026 and change at each monthly refresh. Websites were read on 27 November 2026, with the page rendered as a browser shows it; the evidence for each cell, and where it was found, is on each venue's page. Ties are broken by the Bayesian average, then by the number of reviews.

Our own figures

Our own count of 27 November 2026: of 238 massage venues, 77 publish a price list on a website of their own, 7 state there that English is spoken and 114 are within 1 km of My Khe beach; median published price of a 60-minute body massage: 420K VND (11 venues). Also counted: 105 have a website of their own (38 only a social page), 10 describe a hygiene practice, 200 close at 22:00 or later, and the median distance to the shore is 1.1 km.

How they are counted: every venue in the Google snapshot is counted once. “A website of its own” and the price list, English and hygiene statements come from the website reading of 27 November 2026 (pages rendered as a browser shows them). Distances are straight lines from the Google coordinates to the shore at My Khe, drawn through four points (16.0717, 108.2468 ; 16.0612, 108.2474 ; 16.0526, 108.248 ; 16.0405, 108.2512). Closing times are Monday's hours on Google. The median price is taken from the venues whose own website gives a price for a 60-minute body massage, read by hand where the first match was ambiguous.

The first criterion: a Bayesian average of Google ratings

Each venue's Google rating is weighted by its number of reviews: (v × R + m × C) ÷ (v + m), where R is the Google rating, v the number of Google reviews, C the average rating of all 238 venues (4.79 in this snapshot) and m their median number of reviews (392). In practice a 5.0 from 25 reviews scores 4.80 and sits below a 5.0 from 300, which scores 4.88.

No pick, no placement

This guide has no featured venue. Every list is the score, venue by venue, and Google's own order is published unchanged at /venues/by-google-rating/.
