## The first five, one by one

### 1. Panda Spa Body & Foot Massage

229 Nguyễn Văn Thoại, An Hải, near the beach. Daily 9:00 to 23:45. Google: 4.9 from 3,183. Its [menu](https://pandaspa.vn/) lists foot massage, shiatsu, full-body massage with essential oils, Thai massage and hot stones; a 60-minute relaxing massage is 550,000 VND, tip included. A reviewer: “everyone speaks English very well”. Same company as Panda Relax Spa, on the same street.

### 2. B Spa Bamboo Viet

1 Lê Quang Đạo, Ngũ Hành Sơn. Daily 8:00 to 1:00. Google: 4.9 from 1,547. [Its site](https://bspabambooviet.com/): “Our team and menus speak English, Vietnamese, Chinese and Korean”; a 60-minute traditional Vietnamese body massage with oil and acupressure is 420,000 VND; bamboo and hot stone massage on the same menu.

### 3. Panda Relax Spa

225c Nguyễn Văn Thoại, An Hải. Daily 9:00 to 23:45. Google: 4.9 from 369. Same menu and prices as Panda Spa; its site states that “all tools, towels, and robes are thoroughly cleaned and sterilized after each use”. A reviewer: “Good English at reception”.

### 4. Reborn Nails & Retreat

56 Châu Thị Vĩnh Tế, My An, 880 m from the beach. Daily 9:00 to 20:00. Google: 4.9 from 386. A nail salon and head spa whose [menu](https://rebornnaildanang.com/services/massage/) prices a 60-minute full body massage (340,000 VND), foot and calf massage (15 or 30 minutes), herbal head spa rituals of 25 to 105 minutes and hot stones, with their minutes. **Points lost:** no Thai or shiatsu on its menu (3 points). On the four rankings by treatment (foot massage, head spa, hot stones, near the beach) it comes third.

### 5. Nón Spa

43 An Thượng 3, An Thượng. Google: 4.9 from 704. [Its menu](https://nonspa.vn/) lists Vietnamese, aroma, herbal, hot stone, four-hands and Thai massage; its FAQ: “English of our receptionists are quite good”. **Points lost:** no hygiene practice described on its site, and Google lists its hours for fewer than seven days.
